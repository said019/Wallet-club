# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AgendaFull speaks to owners and operators of fitness and wellness studios, especially pilates, yoga, barre, cycling, dance, and boutique training spaces. They run class-based businesses where the student experience depends on reservations, memberships, reminders, payments, attendance, and recurring visits feeling effortless.

The primary visitor is evaluating whether AgendaFull can understand their real operation and create a polished digital system without forcing them into a generic booking template.

## Product Purpose

AgendaFull is a studio/team that designs and builds custom digital systems for businesses that depend on reservations, memberships, orders, payments, communication, and repeat customers.

The first market focus is fitness and wellness. Success means a visitor quickly understands that AgendaFull can make their student experience feel premium while also making the business easier to operate.

## Brand Personality

AgendaFull should feel premium, clear, and capable. It should speak with warmth and confidence, using "we" as a studio/team.

The voice is direct and practical. Avoid heavy technical jargon, exaggerated SaaS claims, or language that sounds like a cheap template service.

## Anti-references

AgendaFull should not look or sound like a generic teal SaaS landing page, a loyalty-card-only product, a booking widget reseller, or a cold software agency.

Avoid repeated centered icon-card grids, fake metrics, vague AI-style copy, and overused green/cream wellness design.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the student experience, then prove the operational value behind it.
2. Show systems as connected flows, not isolated features.
3. Make AgendaFull feel custom and consultative without becoming vague.
4. Use fitness and wellness as the main proof point, then show adaptability to other business types.
5. Keep the page easy to act on, with WhatsApp as the clear conversion path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and readable Spanish copy across desktop and mobile. Use motion subtly and make the experience usable without relying on animation. Avoid tiny text, low-contrast gray, and layout patterns that clip or overlap on mobile.
